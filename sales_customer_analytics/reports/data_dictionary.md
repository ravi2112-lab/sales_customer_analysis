# Data Dictionary: Sales & Customer Analytics Dataset

> **Dataset Classification**: Synthetic Retail & E-Commerce Benchmark Dataset  
> **Record Count**: 7,200 Clean Transactions (7,225 Raw)  
> **Temporal Coverage**: January 1, 2023 – December 31, 2025  
> **Source**: Algorithmic generation (`src/generate_data.py`) with reproducible seed (`seed=42`)

---

## 1. Overview & Disclaimer

This dataset represents a realistic multi-year transactional record of an omnichannel commercial retailer operating across the United States. All customer names, order identifiers, and financial values are **100% synthetic** and designed specifically for exploratory data analysis, relational SQL modeling, KPI calculation, and interactive BI dashboarding.

## 5. Derived Business Metrics & Formula Glossary

1. **Gross Revenue**:  
   $$\text{Gross Revenue} = \text{unit\_price} \times \text{quantity}$$

2. **Net Sales Revenue**:  
   $$\text{Net Sales Revenue} = \text{Gross Revenue} \times (1 - \text{discount})$$

3. **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)**:  
   $$\text{COGS} = \text{unit\_cost} \times \text{quantity}$$

4. **Net Profit**:  
   $$\text{Net Profit} = \text{Net Sales Revenue} - \text{COGS}$$

5. **Profit Margin (%)**:  
   $$\text{Profit Margin} = \left(\frac{\text{Net Profit}}{\text{Net Sales Revenue}}\right) \times 100$$

6. **Average Order Value (AOV)**:  
   $$\text{AOV} = \frac{\sum \text{Net Sales Revenue}}{\text{Distinct Order Count}}$$
